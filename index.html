About

I'm a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Northeastern University's Silicon Valley campus, working as an AI/ML developer in the Physical Hardware Intelligence Lab and doing thesis research on constrained multi-agent reinforcement learning.

My path into robotics went through cars. As an undergraduate in Computer Engineering at Uludağ University I built perception for an autonomous research vehicle, then joined Renault's ADAS team, where the drowsiness-detection model I worked on shipped in a production car. That taught me what it takes to move a model from a notebook to hardware people rely on: latency budgets, false-alarm rates, and testing that reflects the real world.

Today I bring that mindset to robot learning: clean teleoperation data, honest real-robot evaluation, and policies fast enough to run at control rate. I'm most excited about robot foundation models, sim-to-real transfer, and perception for robots that share space with people.
